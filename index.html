Questions, answered.

What happens at the visit?
+

Your city coordinator comes to your parents' home, introduces themself, and explains how Nityaseva works — regular nurse visits, doctor on call and companion visits. They answer every question your parents may have, with no pressure to buy anything.

Why only ₹11?
+

It is our Diwali shagun. The ₹11 holds your slot and tells us you really want the visit, so our coordinators' time goes to families who are expecting them.

How do the 25 slots work?
+

Each city has 25 slots. They go to the first 25 bookings in the order they arrive. Once filled, a city's slots are closed for this offer.

When will the coordinator come?
+

After you book, your city coordinator will call you within 24–48 hours to confirm a time that works for your parents, any time between 1 and 20 November.

What is a good Diwali gift for elderly parents?
+

The best gift is often time and attention — someone visiting them, checking on their health, and being available when needed. A Nityaseva home visit gives your parents exactly that, explained in person.

Diwali par mummy papa ke liye kya gift dein?
+

Is Diwali, mummy papa ko ek visit gift kijiye. Hamare city coordinator Asansol ya Burnpur mein unke ghar aakar milenge, sab kuch aaram se samjhayenge aur unke saare sawaalon ka jawab denge.

Can I book for both my parents, or for more than one home?
+

Yes. One booking covers one home visit at one address. You can make more than one booking if you'd like visits at different homes.
