Questions, answered.

What happens at the visit?
+

Your city coordinator comes to your parents' home, introduces themself, and explains how Nityaseva works — regular nurse visits, doctor on call and companion visits. They answer every question your parents may have, with no pressure to buy anything.

Why only ₹11?
+

₹11 is a small token to confirm your booking and prevent no-shows, so our coordinators can plan their day efficiently and give your parents proper time and attention.

How do the 25 slots work?
+

Each city has 25 slots. They go to the first 25 bookings whose ₹11 payment comes through, in the order the payments arrive. Once filled, a city's slots are closed for this offer.

When will the coordinator come?
+

After you book, your city coordinator will call you within 24–48 hours to confirm a time that works for your parents, any time between 1 and 20 November.

What is a good Diwali gift for elderly parents?
+

The best gift is often time and attention — someone visiting them, checking on their health, and being available when needed. A Nityaseva home visit gives your parents exactly that, explained in person.

Diwali par mummy papa ke liye kya gift dein?
×

Is Diwali, mummy papa ko ek visit gift kijiye. Sirf ₹11 mein Nityaseva ke city coordinator Lucknow, Nagpur ya Bangalore mein unke ghar aakar milenge, sab kuch aaram se samjhayenge aur unke saare sawaalon ka jawab denge.

Can I book for both my parents, or for more than one home?
+

Yes. One booking covers one home visit at one address. You can make more than one booking if you'd like visits at different homes, each for ₹11.

Offer terms

Bookings are open until 20 November 2026 (IST), in Lucknow, Nagpur and Bangalore. Visits happen between 1 and 20 November.
